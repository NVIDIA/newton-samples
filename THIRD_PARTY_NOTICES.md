Upstream reference files:

included in this repository's [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Runtime-downloaded Franka robot asset

The example loads the FR3 URDF and meshes from Newton's `franka_emika_panda` asset
using `newton.utils.download_asset`. These asset files are downloaded on demand;
they are not bundled in this repository or its Python package.

- Asset collection: [newton-physics/newton-assets](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton-assets).
- Revision used by the locked Newton 1.6.0 dependency: `f8fb7abcbeba2318814a74f3eeb02780ad7925d6`.
- [Asset source at that revision](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton-assets/tree/f8fb7abcbeba2318814a74f3eeb02780ad7925d6/franka_emika_panda).
- License: Apache-2.0 with an additional Willow Garage BSD notice.
- The complete upstream license and additional notice are preserved verbatim in
  [licenses/franka_emika_panda-LICENSE.txt](licenses/franka_emika_panda-LICENSE.txt).

The pulley, cable, and weight are generated by the example. Future assets should
be added to these notices with their sources, revisions, and licenses.
